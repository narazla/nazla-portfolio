Hi, I'm Nara

I'm a Jakarta-based Software Engineer. My work sits at the intersection of clean systems and thoughtful interfaces, shaped by time spent on both sides of the build: writing the code and designing what people actually see.

In December 2025, I took first place nationally at HackAttack in Bandung for ExCellera, a platform helping formerly incarcerated people find steady work again. Designing for users who'd been let down by systems before taught me to treat every screen as a promise you have to keep, not just a layout to fill.

I care about software that holds up under pressure just as much as I care about how it feels to use, and that habit of looking both ways is what I bring into every project.

Let's create something bold together.
